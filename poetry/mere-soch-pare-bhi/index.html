Mere Soch Pare Bhi, Kuch Hona Hain

Ek samay tha jahan, Sab kuch naa paane ka bhay tha Ek aaj waqt hain, jaha sab kuch hoke bhi Jindgi, lagti kamwaqt hain!!! Kuch paya, toh kuch khoya hu Kuch paya, toh kuch khoya hu Dil jab jab na laga, tab tab roya hu. Sapne dekhe bade udaan ke hain, Par main toh yunhi soya hu. Dil jab jab na laga, tab tab roya hu. Pata hota agar, kuch paane ko, kuch khona hain! Mere सोच pare bhi, kuch hona hain. Sambhaal ke rakhata uss pal ko, jo khaas the Jab pata hota isko bhi, ek din khona hain. Mere soch pare bhi, kuch hona hain.
1842
