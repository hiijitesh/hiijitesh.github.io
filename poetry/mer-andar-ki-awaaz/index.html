Mere Andar Ki Awaaz

mere andar ki awaj kuch kahati hain main sun raha hu sab, ye mujh se kuch puchhati hain ja raha hun kaha main, ye mujhko tatol’ti hain kaha galat kaha sahi ye bina dare bolti hain main kar saka na, kar paya kya uss soch me dhakelti hain agr bata doon yuhi taal matol ke fir ye mujhko koshati hain chupa chap main rahu toh kuch toh mere se bolti hain
