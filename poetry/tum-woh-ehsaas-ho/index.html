Tum Woh Ehsaas Ho

jo pal pal mere saath ho kabhi sochta hu ek din kaise katega tum bin, magar sach toh ye hain ki ek pal bhi sadiyo sa lagea 7 janam sa ek ek din tum woh aadat ban gayi ho jisko main gum me khushi me jeev jantu sab me dhudhata hun guzaara nahi hota mera tum bin ek pal, kyuki sahaaraa ho tum mere pal pal ka likhana chahatu bahut kuch tumhare baare me magar keh du kya tumhe dundha nahi main Chand sitaaro me ek chhoti si aarzu hain, tum hamesha raho meri baaho me kalaa ki kami hain, nahi toh likh du tumpe woh nazam, shayri aur kitaab magar tumhe dekh ke sab bhool jata hu kuchh iss tarah pyaar hua hain aap se janbab

Jitesh Giri

Poet & Storyteller • Pure dil se likha hua
