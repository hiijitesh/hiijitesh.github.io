Kuch Khayaal Aise Hain

Kuch khayal aise hain jo meet nahi patein Kuch shaam aisi hain jo dhal nahi patein Log toh charo taraf hain Lekin fir sab me hum ghool meel nahi patein Bahar bheed ka nazaraa hain toh Under jhanka toh sukha kinaara hain Kuch khayal aise hain jo meet nahi patein Kuch shaam aisi hain jo dhal nahi patein Kabhi aarzoo hoti hain apno ki, Fir lagta sab thik ho jayegaa Par ye toh waqat he batayega, Lagta hain ye zindgi yuhi, Tanha kat jayega Sapne toh hazaar hain, Bahut he besoomar hain Sapno ko pana hain ya apno ko, Ye soch ke dil av bhi bekaraar hain Anshu toh dil me aatein hain, Ankho ki aukaat kya hain Sochata hu ro lu jee bhar ke, Par itna v ye aashaan kahan hain Kuch khayal aise hain jo meet nahi patein Kuch shaam aisi hain jo dhal nahi patein

Jitesh Bharti

Poet & Storyteller • Pure dil se likha hua
